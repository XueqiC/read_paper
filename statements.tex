\subsection*{AI use statement}

In this work, we used generative AI tools to assist with implementing parts of
the experimental code (e.g., data processing, evaluation, and plotting scripts)
and to improve the writing of the manuscript (grammar, wording, and
formatting). We have not used generative AI tools to generate synthetic
datasets, develop the theoretical model or conceptual framework, formulate or
prove mathematical claims, propose or refine hypotheses, design the research
methodology or experiments, or interpret results; the remaining tasks with
required disclosure (e.g., translation and qualitative or thematic data
analysis) are not applicable to this work. We have reviewed all AI-assisted
work: AI-assisted code was checked and tested by the authors before use, and
all AI-edited text was verified by the authors against the intended content.
We take responsibility for the final content of this work, including text,
claims, and artifacts produced with the aid of generative AI.

\subsection*{Ethics statement}

This work does not involve human subjects, crowdsourcing, or personally
identifiable data. All experiments use publicly available pretrained models and
public benchmarks, and evaluation is fully automated. ReAD is a method for
allocating a fixed distillation budget across capabilities; it does not
introduce new datasets or release new model weights. As with any distillation
method, distilled students may inherit biases or unsafe behaviors from the
teacher and the training data, and more efficient distillation may lower the
cost of producing specialized models with dual-use capabilities. We discuss
these risks and the corresponding mitigations (task-specific safety evaluation,
bias and robustness audits, and monitoring of off-target capability changes) in
Appendix~\ref{sec:broader_impact}.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}

We have made efforts to ensure that our results are reproducible.
Section~\ref{subsec:exp_setup} specifies the teacher--student model pairs,
token budgets, evaluation protocol, baselines, and the ReAD update schedule,
and Section~\ref{sec:method} describes each component of ReAD.
Appendix~\ref{sec:bench_datasets} lists the benchmarks, metrics, and data
splits used for each capability, Appendix~\ref{sec:app_em} details the setting
of the exploratory study, and Appendix~\ref{sec:app_experiment} reports
additional experiments and ablations. The assumptions and full derivations for
the theoretical results in Section~\ref{sec:theory} are given in
Appendix~\ref{sec:app_theory}. All reported numbers are means and standard
deviations over three seeds. An anonymized repository with our code,
configuration files, and instructions for reproducing the main results is
available at \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/ReAD-85EB}.
