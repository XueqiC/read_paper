\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:con}

\rev{We study how to allocate a fixed distillation budget across capability-targeted supervision to improve a specified target capability. ReAD combines a task-conditioned allocation prior, on-the-fly teacher supervision, and an uncertainty-aware contextual bandit. Under the same budget, it reaches higher on-target scores than the target-only baselines in our experiments and improves over static and greedy schedules with a matched generator. Its off-target declines are also smaller than those of single-capability distillation. Two limits remain. The proxy reward may deviate from true task utility, and the efficiency figures are interpolation-based estimates from two budgets. Validating the proxy against downstream utility, measuring more budget points, and pairing the allocator with on-policy distillation losses are natural next steps.}
